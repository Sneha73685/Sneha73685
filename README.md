I'm an engineering student focused on **AI systems, data visualization, and motorsport analytics**.

I enjoy building systems where software, data, and real-world decision-making meet — from Formula 1 telemetry and race-performance research to computer vision, multilingual AI, and interactive applications.

- Data-driven decision systems

I'm particularly interested in the space between **research and engineering** — taking difficult problems, extracting useful signals from complex data, and turning those signals into systems people can actually use.
